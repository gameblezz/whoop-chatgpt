Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026

Kanasin WHOOP ChatGPT is a personal integration intended to allow the account owner to access and analyze their own WHOOP data through an AI assistant.

Information Accessed

With the user's explicit authorization, the application may access WHOOP data permitted by the scopes selected during authorization, including profile, body measurements, physiological cycles, recovery, sleep, and workout information.

How Information Is Used

WHOOP data is used only to retrieve, display, summarize, and analyze the authorized user's personal fitness and wellness information in response to their requests.

Data Sharing and Sale

This application does not sell personal information. It does not intentionally share WHOOP data with third parties except for services necessary to operate the integration and process requests initiated by the authorized user.

Data Storage and Security

OAuth credentials and tokens should be stored securely and used only to access the authorized user's WHOOP account. The integration is intended for personal use. Reasonable measures are used to protect credentials and authorized data.

User Control

The user may revoke the application's access to their WHOOP account at any time through the applicable WHOOP account or authorization settings. Stored credentials associated with the integration may also be deleted by the account owner.

Contact

For privacy questions regarding this personal integration, contact the application owner using the contact information provided in the WHOOP Developer application configuration.

Changes

This policy may be updated as the integration changes. Updates will be published on this page with a revised date.
